⚡ Power supply rule of thumb

One 3090: good 850W (1000W for high-TDP Threadripper). Two stock 3090s: good 1200–1500W; 1500–1600W offers more headroom on Threadripper. Budget ~£70–130 for a complete used 850W unit; a used HX1500i was listed at ~£197, so budget ~£200–330 for a sound high-wattage unit.

🧠 The RAM + X299 trap

X99 DDR3 is NOT universal—many X99 boards use DDR4 only, and 128GB often requires eight supported DIMM sockets. RDIMM and UDIMM cannot be interchanged. Some WRX80 boards accept either type—check the motherboard manual. Budget 128GB DDR4 ECC RDIMM 2400–2666 ~£370–580; 3200 kits can cost more. X299 needs a 44/48-lane CPU such as i9-10900X for x16/x16; cheaper 16/28-lane CPUs may reduce GPU bandwidth. Physical slot spacing and NVLink fit are separate checks.

How to read prices: indicative board + CPU + 128GB total RAM + suitable PSU, excluding graphics cards, case, storage, cooler and shipping. Estimates are not a lowest-price guarantee; eBay asks and completed listings are mixed. Bundles containing some RAM are priced conservatively as if buying a separate 128GB kit, so totals may overstate actual upgrade spend. Physical GPU spacing, power connectors and NVLink software support must be verified per board.
